Best results come from clear instructions, like "Summarize this page in 5 bullet points for a beginner."
</Info>

## Why use Dume for Chrome

Dume for Chrome understands your current tab and helps you:

- Summarize articles, docs, and reports quickly
- Draft responses in tools like Gmail
- Extract structured data from websites
- Ask questions about the page you're viewing
- Keep research and notes organized with less effort

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    Open the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore), find **Dume for Chrome**, and click **Add to Chrome**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin for one-click access">
    Click the Chrome extensions puzzle icon and pin Dume so it's always visible. Without pinning, you'll need to dig through the extensions menu every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up or log in">
    Open the extension popup and authenticate with Google or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first task">
    Open any long page and ask: "Summarize this in 5 beginner-friendly bullets."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick prompts to try

| Goal | Prompt |
| :--- | :--- |
| Manage tabs | "Group all my open tabs related to travel planning and suggest which ones to close." |
| Compare options | "Compare the features listed across my 5 open product tabs in a table." |
| Understand quickly | "Explain this page in simple terms as if I'm new to this topic." |
| Plan next steps | "Create a 3-step action plan based on this page." |

<Note>
Include audience and format in your prompt for better results, like "for non-technical stakeholders" or "as a table."
</Note>

## Keyboard shortcut

Open the Dume sidebar instantly:
- Mac: `Cmd + Shift + D`
- Windows: `Ctrl + Shift + D`

Customize this anytime at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if the extension isn't opening?">
    Refresh the current tab, confirm the extension is pinned, and check for conflicting extensions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why am I seeing 'Not logged in'?">
    Open the extension popup and sign in again with the same method, Google or email, that you use for the Dume web app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why aren't action buttons showing on some sites?">
    Some complex site layouts load slowly. Refresh the page and make sure it's fully loaded before opening the Dume sidebar.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Start with simple summary prompts to get a feel for the results, then move to research and data-extraction tasks once you trust the output.
</Tip>

## Why teams keep it enabled

Support teams draft reply options in Gmail without leaving the ticket. Sales and marketing use deep research to prep a call or a competitive brief in minutes instead of a dozen open tabs. Product and ops teams pull structured data out of long docs and reports without retyping it. The common thread: less tab-switching, less manual reading, more of the actual work.
