Dume is an AI executive assistant you talk to like a person. Connect your inbox, calendar, and docs, and Dume works from them directly. No separate app to learn for each tool.

Talk to Dume from the [web app](/product/web-app), the [Cowork desktop app](/product/dume-cowork), [WhatsApp](/interfaces/whatsapp), or a [phone call](/interfaces/phone) — same assistant, same context, wherever you reach it from.

## What you can do

- Turn a backlog of unread email into a short brief with drafted replies.
- Research a company or a person before a call and get back a concise brief.
- Pull this week's industry news into one summary instead of five open tabs.
- Set up a [routine](/routines/overview), like a daily briefing or a weekly wrap-up, and let Dume run it on its own from then on.
- Draft a slide deck, write a doc, or clean up a spreadsheet in [Dume Office](/office/overview), the native Docs/Sheets/Slides/PDF editors built into Cowork desktop.
