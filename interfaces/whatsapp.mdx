Access your personal executive AI assistant anywhere, anytime through WhatsApp. Dume.ai integrates seamlessly with WhatsApp, bringing the full power of AI assistance to the messaging platform you already know and love.

<Note>
The WhatsApp interface provides convenient mobile access to your AI assistant. For advanced features like workflow creation and integration management, use the [web interface](/interfaces/web).
</Note>

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login to Web Application">
    Visit [app.dume.ai](https://app.dume.ai) and sign in to your account.
    
    <Info>
    You'll need an active WhatsApp account on your mobile device to complete the connection.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Find WhatsApp Contact">
    In the web application, locate and click on **"Message us on WhatsApp"** to get the Dume.ai WhatsApp number.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Send a Message on WhatsApp">
    Open WhatsApp on your phone and send a message to the Dume.ai number. You can say "Hello" or "Hi" to start.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Link Your Account">
    If your account isn't linked yet, you'll receive an account linking link in WhatsApp. Click the link to connect your Dume.ai account with WhatsApp.
    
    <Check>
    Once linked, your AI assistant is ready to help with tasks, questions, and automations directly in WhatsApp!
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive an account linking link">
    Make sure you're logged in to the web application first and have sent a message to the correct Dume.ai WhatsApp number. If you still don't receive the link, try messaging "Link account" or contact support.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="The account linking link expired or doesn't work">
    Account linking links are time-sensitive for security. Simply send another message on WhatsApp to receive a new linking link.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Not receiving responses after linking">
    Check if you've blocked or archived the Dume.ai chat. Also verify your phone has an active internet connection and that the account linking was successful.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How do I disconnect WhatsApp?">
    Go to the web interface, navigate to Settings > Integrations > WhatsApp, and click "Disconnect." To reconnect, you'll need to follow the linking process again.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I use multiple WhatsApp numbers?">
    Currently, you can connect one WhatsApp account per Dume.ai account. To switch accounts, disconnect the current one and connect a new number.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Does this work with WhatsApp Business?">
    Yes! Dume.ai works with both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is WhatsApp access free?">
    WhatsApp access is included with your Dume.ai subscription at no additional cost.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Do I need the web interface if I use WhatsApp?">
    Yes, you need to login to the web interface first to get the WhatsApp contact and complete the account linking. The web interface is also required for creating workflows and managing integrations. WhatsApp is perfect for day-to-day interactions and quick tasks.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I link WhatsApp without logging into the web app?">
    No, you must first login to the web application to access the "Message us on WhatsApp" option and complete the account linking process.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What languages are supported?">
    Dume.ai supports multiple languages. Send messages in your preferred language, and the assistant will respond accordingly.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can others see my WhatsApp messages to Dume.ai?">
    No, your conversations are private and encrypted. Only you can see messages between you and your AI assistant.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens if I change my phone number?">
    You'll need to disconnect the old WhatsApp connection and set up a new one with your new number through the web interface.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
