WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach Dume when you're not at your desk. Link it once and message the same assistant you use in the app.

{/* TODO: Phase 3 screenshot — Settings, Connectors, WhatsApp Connection card */}

## Linking your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and find **WhatsApp Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. You'll get a QR code to scan, or a `wa.me` link you can open directly on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message">
    Message the Dume WhatsApp number to complete the link. Once it's linked, the card shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
There's a **remote control** toggle on the WhatsApp connection. Turn it off if you want WhatsApp for notifications only, without letting messages trigger actions.
</Tip>

## What you can do

Anything you'd ask in the app: check your calendar, get an inbox summary, ask Dume to research something, or check on a [routine](/routines/overview). WhatsApp doesn't cover everything, mainly account and integration management, which need the [web app](/product/web-app) or [Cowork desktop](/product/dume-cowork).

## Disconnecting

From **Settings → Connectors**, click **Forget Account** on the WhatsApp card. You can reconnect at any time by scanning a new code.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get a response after scanning the code">
    Confirm you sent a message to the Dume number after scanning, not just scanned and closed WhatsApp. Check your connection status under Settings → Connectors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one WhatsApp number?">
    One WhatsApp number links per Dume account. To switch, forget the current connection and link the new number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does this work with WhatsApp Business?">
    Yes, both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts can link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
