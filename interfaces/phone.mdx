Phone calling works two ways: you can call Dume, and Dume can call you.

{/* TODO: Phase 3 screenshot — Settings, Connectors, Phone Calling card */}

## Calling Dume

Dial the Dume phone number from any phone, mobile or landline, and talk to your assistant the way you'd talk to a person. Inbound calling works from any number worldwide; there's no country restriction on calling in.

## Dume calling you

Ask Dume to call you when something finishes, like a background research task or a long-running job, and it calls your linked number with the result. This is also how a **call-user** routine works, like the Daily Standup Call or Evening Wind-Down Call templates.

<Note>
Outbound calling (Dume calling you) currently supports these countries: **United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, India, United Arab Emirates, Germany, France, Brazil, and Japan.** If your number isn't in one of these, you can still call in yourself.
</Note>

## What it costs

A call is billed two ways at once, for as long as it's connected:

1. **A fixed per-minute rate**, charged every minute the call is connected:

   | Direction | Cost |
   |---|---|
   | Calling Dume (inbound) | 5 credits/minute |
   | Dume calling you (outbound) | 8 credits/minute |

2. **An AI usage charge**, billed continuously on top, based on how much is actually said on the call. This works the same way a chat message does: more back-and-forth costs more, a short call costs less. See the [Credit Cost Guide](/account-settings/credit-cost-guide) for how usage-based credit costs work generally.

Both charges apply to every call; the per-minute rate isn't the full cost by itself. You need a minimum credit balance to start a call (a small buffer, not tied to a specific call length); if your balance is too low, the call won't connect. See [Billing & plans](/account-settings/billing-and-plans) for your plan's monthly calling-minute allowance, which is separate from and in addition to the credit cost.

## Setting up your number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors** and find **Phone Calling**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your number">
    Enter your phone number and verify it with the SMS code Dume sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once verified, the card shows **Active** with your number, and both calling directions are ready to use (subject to the country note above).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove your number, click **Remove Number** on the same card.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dume isn't calling me back">
    Check that outbound calling supports your country under Settings → Connectors → Phone Calling. If it doesn't yet, you can still call in directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my existing phone number?">
    Yes. You verify your own number with an SMS code; there's no separate number to set up on your end.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my call get cut off or refuse to connect?">
    Check your credit balance under Settings → Billing. Calls need a minimum balance to start and are billed per minute while connected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
