## How It Will Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Dume.ai Bot">
    Search for @DumeAI (or similar) in Telegram's search bar and start a chat.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Link Your Account">
    Send /start command and follow the prompts to link your Dume.ai account securely.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Begin conversing with your AI assistant or use quick commands for instant actions.

  <Step title="Use Interactive Features">

## Bot Commands (Planned)

Quick commands that will be available:

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/start` | Start the bot and link your account |

| `/tasks` | View your current tasks |
| `/schedule` | View your calendar for today |
| `/email` | Check recent important emails |
| `/workflow [name]` | Trigger a custom workflow |
| `/settings` | Manage bot settings and preferences |
