## How It Will Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Your Assistant Email">
    You'll receive a unique email address for your AI assistant (e.g., assistant@dume.ai).
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Send an Email">
    Simply send an email to your assistant's address from any email client.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Receive AI-Powered Response">
    Your assistant will process your request and reply with answers, confirmations, or completed tasks.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Continue the Conversation">
    Reply to continue the conversation, with full context preserved in the email thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>

    ```

    Priority: High
    Include: Financial summary, team metrics, and goals for Q1
    ```
    
    **Response:**
    ```

    - Financial summary
    ```

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will email interface be available?">
    We're actively developing the email interface and plan to launch it in the coming months. Join the waitlist to be notified when it's ready.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I use my existing email address?">
    You'll send emails to your assistant's dedicated email address from your existing email. No new email account needed.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Which email providers will be supported?">
    The email interface works with any email client or provider (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.) since it's based on standard email protocols.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Will there be size limits on emails?">
    Standard email size limits will apply (typically 25MB including attachments), but we'll support processing large documents.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I forward emails to my assistant?">
    Yes! You'll be able to forward emails to your assistant for summarization, action item extraction, and response drafting.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How fast will responses be?">
    Most simple requests will be answered within minutes. Complex tasks may take longer, and you'll receive progress updates via email.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
