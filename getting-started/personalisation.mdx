## Why Personalize Dume?

Personalization transforms Dume from a generic AI assistant into your knowledgeable partner. By understanding your background, expertise, and preferences, Dume can:

- **Provide contextual responses** tailored to your role and industry
- **Adjust technical depth** based on your experience level
- **Reference your specific interests** and professional focus areas
- **Save time** by eliminating the need to repeatedly explain your context

<Tip>
The more information you provide, the better Dume becomes at understanding your needs and providing relevant assistance.
</Tip>

## Prerequisites

Before personalizing Dume, ensure you have:
- An active Dume account
- Basic profile information (name, role, interests)
- Optional: Website URL or social media profiles for auto-research

## What Happens Next?

After completing personalization:

1. **Immediate Impact**: Dume begins incorporating your profile information into its responses
2. **Learning Process**: The AI continues to learn from your interactions and feedback
3. **Ongoing Refinement**: You can update your profile as your interests or role evolve

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I don't see the personalization option">
  Ensure you're logged into your Dume account and have the latest version. The personalization feature may not be available in all regions or account types.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Auto-research isn't finding information">
  - Verify your URL is correct and publicly accessible
  - Ensure your profiles contain relevant professional information
  - Try different profile URLs (LinkedIn, personal website, etc.)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Responses aren't more personalized">
  - Give Dume a few interactions to learn from your new profile
  - Provide more specific information in your profile
  - Try asking questions related to your stated expertise areas
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
