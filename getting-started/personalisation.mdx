## Why Personalize Dume?

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contextual Responses" icon="sparkles">
    Tailors answers to your role, industry, and current focus areas instead of generic output.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Right Technical Depth" icon="gear-complex-code">
    Adjusts complexity (high-level vs detailed) based on your stated experience level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Relevant Interests" icon="bullseye-pointer">
    Weaves in your specialty domains (e.g. activation, onboarding) when generating suggestions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Time Savings" icon="clock">
    Removes repetitive re‑explaining of background; persistent profile informs every interaction.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Adding precise role + focus areas (e.g. “B2B SaaS PM, activation funnels”) improves relevance across workflows and agents.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Personalization Settings">
    Navigate to the personalization menu to begin customizing your Dume experience.

        <img
            className="block dark:hidden"

    The personalization option is typically found in the main menu or settings area of your Dume interface.

  <Step title="Complete Your Profile Information">
    Provide essential details about yourself to help Dume understand your background and expertise.

      <Frame>
          <img
              className="block dark:hidden"
              src="/images/light/input-modal.png"
              alt="Dume profile input form showing fields for name, role, interests, and background information"
              title="Profile information input form"

              className="hidden dark:block"

      </Frame>
      
      **Key information to include:**
      - Your name and professional title
      - Industry or field of expertise
      - Areas of interest or specialization
      - Experience level in relevant topics
      
      <Tip>
      Be specific about your role and interests. Instead of "developer," try "full-stack developer specializing in React and Node.js."
      </Tip>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enable Auto-Research (Optional)">
    Let Dume automatically gather additional context about you from your online presence.

      <Frame>
          <img
              className="block dark:hidden"
              src="/images/light/auto-research.png"
              alt="Auto-research toggle and input field for website or social media profile URL"
              title="Auto-research feature configuration"
          />
          <img
              className="hidden dark:block"
              src="/images/dark/auto-research.png"
              alt="Auto-research toggle and input field for website or social media profile URL (dark mode)"
              title="Auto-research feature configuration (dark mode)"
          />
      </Frame>
      
      **Auto-research can gather information from:**
      - Your personal or professional website
      - LinkedIn profile
      - GitHub profile
      - Other publicly available professional profiles
      
      <Warning>
      Only provide URLs to public profiles you're comfortable having analyzed. Dume will only access publicly available information.
      </Warning>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Save and Confirm Your Preferences">
    Review your personalization settings and save them to activate your customized Dume experience.
    
    <Check>
    Once saved, Dume will immediately begin using your profile information to provide more relevant and personalized responses.
    </Check>
    
    <Note>
    You can update your personalization settings at any time to refine Dume's understanding of your preferences and expertise.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next?

After completing personalization:

1. **Immediate Impact**: Dume begins incorporating your profile information into its responses
2. **Learning Process**: The AI continues to learn from your interactions and feedback
3. **Ongoing Refinement**: You can update your profile as your interests or role evolve

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I don't see the personalization option">
  Ensure you're logged into your Dume account and have the latest version. The personalization feature may not be available in all regions or account types.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Auto-research isn't finding information">
  - Verify your URL is correct and publicly accessible
  - Ensure your profiles contain relevant professional information
  - Try different profile URLs (LinkedIn, personal website, etc.)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Responses aren't more personalized">
  - Give Dume a few interactions to learn from your new profile
  - Provide more specific information in your profile
  - Try asking questions related to your stated expertise areas
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
