Every session you have with Dume, whether you started it or a [routine](/routines/overview) ran it on a schedule, is saved to History in the sidebar.

{/* TODO: Phase 3 screenshot — sidebar History list */}

Click any entry to reopen it as a new tab, picking up exactly where it left off, including a completed routine's full output. Use **View All** to see the complete list rather than just the most recent few, and the three-dot menu on an entry to pin it to the top, rename it, or delete it for good. There's no cap on how many sessions History holds and nothing gets cleared automatically, so pruning old ones is on you.

See [Cross-session memory](/do-work-with-it/cross-session-memory) for asking Dume about a session from inside a different one, and [Routines](/routines/overview) if what you actually want is Dume doing the same thing on a recurring schedule, not just organizing past chats.
