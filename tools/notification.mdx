## Overview

The notification tool allows you to send email alerts as part of your workflow automation. As your AI executive assistant, Dume.ai can send notifications to keep you informed about important updates, deadlines, and workflow completions.

<Info>
This tool is designed for alerting and workflow automation, not for general email communication. Emails are sent from Dume.ai's domain.
</Info>

<Warning>
Notifications are sent from Dume.ai's domain and should not be used for external business communications or client outreach.
</Warning>

## Current Limitations

<Note>
**Non-Proactive Notifications**: Dume.ai currently cannot send future-scheduled alerts independently. Notifications are triggered by:
- Workflow completions
- Manual activation via chat
- Immediate alert requests
- Status change events
</Note>
