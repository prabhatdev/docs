## Overview

The notification tool allows you to send email alerts as part of your workflow automation. As your AI executive assistant, Dume.ai can send notifications to keep you informed about important updates, deadlines, and workflow completions.

<Info>
This tool is designed for alerting and workflow automation, not for general email communication. Emails are sent from Dume.ai's domain.
</Info>

## How It Works

<Steps>
<Step title="Activate the Tool">
Type `@send_notification` in the chat interface to activate the notification tool.
</Step>

<Step title="Describe Your Alert">
Explain what notification you want to send and when, using natural language.

<CodeGroup>
```bash Daily Briefing Alert
@send_notification Send me a daily briefing summary every morning at 9 AM with today's priorities
```

```bash Task Completion Alert
@send_notification Alert me when the quarterly report workflow is completed
```

```bash Deadline Reminder
@send_notification Remind me 24 hours before the client presentation deadline
```
</CodeGroup>

<Check>
Dume.ai will process your request and send the notification as specified.
</Check>
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Notifications are sent from Dume.ai's domain and should not be used for external business communications or client outreach.
</Warning>


<Accordion title="Milestone Notifications">

## Current Limitations

<Note>
**Non-Proactive Notifications**: Dume.ai currently cannot send future-scheduled alerts independently. Notifications are triggered by:
- Workflow completions
- Manual activation via chat
- Immediate alert requests
- Status change events
</Note>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**Effective Notification Strategy**
- Be specific about timing and triggers
- Include context for what actions to take
- Use for high-priority items only
- Integrate with existing workflows
- Keep notification content actionable
</Tip>

### Do's and Don'ts

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="✅ Recommended Uses" icon="check">
- **Workflow automation**: Task completion alerts
- **Time management**: Schedule and deadline reminders  
- **Progress tracking**: Milestone and goal notifications
- **Personal productivity**: Daily/weekly summaries
- **Business operations**: Performance threshold alerts
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="❌ Not Recommended" icon="x">
- **Client communication**: Use your regular email for external contacts
- **Team collaboration**: Use proper business email for team communications
- **Marketing outreach**: Not suitable for promotional emails
- **Formal business correspondence**: Use your business domain
- **Sensitive information**: Avoid confidential data in notifications
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
