## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Connection Issues">
  **Common problems and solutions:**
  
  **OAuth authentication failed:**
  - Clear browser cookies and try again in incognito mode
  - Ensure you're logged into the correct email account
  - Check if third-party app access is enabled in your email settings
  - For Microsoft365: Verify enterprise app approval policies
  
  **Integration shows connected but doesn't work:**
  - Test with a simple query like "show me recent emails"
  - Check if permissions were modified after initial connection
  - Verify email account is active and accessible
  - Reconnect the integration to refresh permissions
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Performance Issues">
  **Slow email responses or timeouts:**
  
  - **Large mailboxes** can take longer to search - be specific in queries
  - **Rate limiting** may occur with frequent requests - wait and retry
  - **Network issues** can cause delays - check internet connection
  - **Email server problems** - verify email provider service status
  
  **Optimization tips:**
  - Use date ranges in searches: "emails from last week" instead of "all emails"
  - Specify folders: "emails in inbox" rather than searching all folders
  - Limit results: "show me 10 most recent emails" for faster responses
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Permission and Access Errors">
  **Authorization problems:**
  
  **Gmail errors:**
  - "Insufficient permissions" - Reconnect and approve all requested scopes
  - "API quota exceeded" - Wait for quota reset or contact support for higher limits
  - "Account access blocked" - Check Gmail security settings for third-party apps
  
  **Microsoft365 errors:**
  - "Admin approval required" - Contact IT team to approve Dume application
  - "Conditional access blocked" - Work with IT to configure access policies
  - "Multi-factor authentication" - Complete MFA setup in Microsoft portal
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Feature Limitations">
  **Understanding integration boundaries:**
  
  **What Dume can do:**
  ✅ Read and search emails across all folders
  ✅ Compose and send emails with AI assistance
  ✅ Organize emails with labels and folders
  ✅ Access basic contact information
  
  **What Dume cannot do:**
  ❌ Modify email server settings or configurations
  ❌ Access emails marked as highly confidential (depending on settings)
  ❌ Perform bulk operations without confirmation
  ❌ Access other users' emails in shared environments
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
