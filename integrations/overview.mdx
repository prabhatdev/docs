Dume connects to your other tools over OAuth. You approve the connection on the tool's own login page, and Dume never sees your password. Every connection can be revoked at any time.

Currently supported: [Gmail](/integrations/gmail), [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar), [Google Docs](/integrations/google_docs), [Google Sheets](/integrations/google_sheets), [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive), [GitHub](/integrations/github), [Slack](/integrations/slack), [Notion](/integrations/notion), and [Microsoft 365](/integrations/microsoft_365). Need something that isn't on this list? Connect it yourself through [MCP Servers](/integrations/mcp/overview).

Free plans have a limit on how many integrations and accounts you can connect at once; upgrading raises that limit.
