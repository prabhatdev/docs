<Note>Part of [Dume Office](/office/overview), currently in **beta**.</Note>

Dume Slides is a native `.pptx` presentation editor in Cowork desktop. It opens and saves real PowerPoint files on your machine, and Dume's AI copilot is built into the editor itself.

## Opening a deck

- **Ask in chat**: "Create a slide deck about our Q3 launch" opens Dume Slides with a first draft, slides and all.
- **New File** inside Dume Office for a blank deck.
- **Open an existing `.pptx`** from Cowork; it launches in Dume Slides already editable.

See [Dume Office](/office/overview) for all three entry points in detail.

## What the copilot can do

Because the copilot has the deck open, it can do anything you'd otherwise do by hand: generate a full deck from a prompt, add or reorder slides, rewrite the copy on a specific slide, or redesign the layout. Point it at a specific slide number rather than "fix the deck" for a tighter result.

```
Create a slide deck about our Q3 launch, 8 slides
Add a slide after slide 3 covering competitive positioning
Rewrite slide 5 to lead with the number, not the narrative
Make the title slide match our brand colors
Condense this into a 5-minute version
```

## Where it fits with the rest of Dume Office

Dume Slides is `.pptx`-only; there's no separate cloud integration for it the way Docs and Sheets have Google equivalents. If your source material lives in Google Docs or Sheets, pull it into a Dume session first, then ask Dume to turn it into a deck in Dume Slides.
