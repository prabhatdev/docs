## How to Use Workflow Templates

### 1. Navigating to the Workflow Templates

- Click on the **"Workflow"** tab in the main navigation menu on the left side of the screen.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.21.23PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.21.23 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.21.23 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"73%" }}
  />
- This will open the Workflow Studio, which displays a list of available workflow templates.

### 2. Selecting a Template

- Scroll through the list of templates to find the one that fits your task. In this example, we're using the **"Company Research"** template.
- Click on the template to view its details. A pop-up window will appear showing a diagram of the workflow's structure and a description of what it does.

  ![Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.22.45 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.22.45PM.png)
- Review the workflow's structure to understand how the different nodes (tasks) are connected. This template, for instance, uses several nodes to gather information, like **"Find News," "Fetch Funding,"** and **"Find Competitors."**

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.24.34PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.24.34 PM.png"
  title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.24.34 PM.png"
  style={{ width:"100%" }}
/>

### 3. Running the Workflow

- Once you've reviewed the template and are ready to run it, click the **"Run"** button in the top-right corner of the screen.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.25.20PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.25.20 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.25.20 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"41%" }}
  />
- A **"Test Run"** pop-up will appear, prompting you to enter the required **"Input."** For the Company Research template, this input is the **"company_domain"** you want to research.
- Type in the company's domain (e.g., "[dume.ai](http://dume.ai)").
- Click **"Run Workflow."**
- The workflow will start running, and you'll see the status of each node change from "Pending" to "Running" and then to "Success" as it completes each task.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.26.09PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.26.09 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.26.09 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"49%" }}
  />

### 4. Viewing the Results

- After the workflow finishes, the **"Test Run"** pop-up will show the **"Result."**
- This result is a summarized output from the workflow, combining the information gathered from all the different nodes into a single, comprehensive document.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.26.58PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.26.58 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.26.58 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"76%" }}
  />
- You can expand the result to see a more detailed breakdown of the information, including an overview of the company, employee details, and recent news.
- You can click on the individual nodes in the **"Test Run"** pop-up (e.g., "Company Summary," "Additional Summary") to see the specific output from that particular step.

### 5. Publishing and Scheduling the Workflow

- If you're happy with the results and want to make the workflow available for repeated use, click the **"Publish"** button.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.28.22PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.28.22 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.28.22 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"48%" }}
  />
- Once published, you can also **schedule** the workflow to run automatically at a specific date and time.
- Click the **"Schedule"** button and enter the required details, such as the schedule name, run date, and time.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2025-09-07at8.29.07PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.29.07 PM.png"
    title="Screenshot 2025-09-07 at 8.29.07 PM.png"
    style={{ width:"31%" }}
  />
- This is useful for tasks that need to be performed regularly, like daily or weekly reports.
