Email Triage is **enabled by default** when you connect your email account to Dume.ai. It runs automatically in the background, keeping your inbox organized without any manual effort.
</Note>

<Info>
Custom instructions for Email Triage are coming soon! You'll be able to provide specific guidance on how you want your emails categorized.
</Info>

## How It Works

When an email arrives in your inbox, Dume.ai's Email Triage system:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Analyzes the Email">
    Dume.ai reads the email's subject, content, sender, and context to understand its purpose and importance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Determines the Best Label">
    Using advanced AI, it matches the email against 19 predefined categories or your existing custom labels to find the perfect fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Applies the Label Automatically">
    The system either uses an existing label from your email client or creates a new one from the taxonomy, keeping everything organized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keeps Learning">
    Over time, Email Triage adapts to your preferences and labeling patterns for even better accuracy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting Started

Email Triage is automatically enabled when you connect your email account—no setup required! Here's how to verify it's working:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your Email">
    Connect your Gmail or other email account to Dume.ai from the [Integrations](https://app.dume.ai/integrations) page.
    <Check>
    Email Triage is now automatically active and will begin labeling new incoming emails.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify It's Working">
    Check your email inbox after a few minutes. New emails should have labels applied automatically based on their content and context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and Adjust">

  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Disable Email Triage

If you prefer to manage your inbox manually or want to pause Email Triage temporarily:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to System Workflows">
    Go to the [Workflows](https://app.dume.ai/workflow) page in Dume.ai and select the **System Workflows** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Email Triage">
    Locate the **Email Triage** workflow in the list of system workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Off">
    Click the toggle switch or **Disable** button to turn off automatic email labeling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your emails will no longer be automatically labeled. You can re-enable it anytime by following the same steps and toggling it back on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
When you disable Email Triage, new incoming emails will not be automatically labeled. Previously labeled emails will keep their labels.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will Email Triage work with my existing labels?">
    Yes! Email Triage intelligently uses your existing labels when appropriate and only creates new ones from the taxonomy when needed.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I customize the labeling taxonomy?">
    Currently, Email Triage uses the predefined 19-category taxonomy, which covers most use cases. **Custom instructions are coming soon**, allowing you to provide specific guidance on how you want your emails categorized.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Does this work with email providers other than Gmail?">
    Email Triage currently supports Gmail and Google Workspace accounts. Support for Microsoft 365 and other providers is in development.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What happens to emails before I connected my account?">
    Email Triage only processes new incoming emails after your account is connected. You can manually run it on existing emails using the workflow editor.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Is my email data secure?">
    Absolutely. Dume.ai uses OAuth for secure authentication and never stores your email content. All processing happens in real-time with encryption.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I turn off Email Triage if I don't want it?">
    Yes! Email Triage is enabled by default but can be easily disabled at any time from the System Workflows section. See the "How to Disable Email Triage" section above for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
