Morning Briefing is an intelligent system workflow that delivers a personalized daily digest to your email every morning between 7:30-8:00 AM. Start your day informed with everything you need to know—weather, schedule, urgent emails, and curated news—all compiled by your AI assistant.

<Note>
Morning Briefing is **enabled by default** when you connect your Gmail account to Dume.ai. You'll start receiving your daily digest automatically.
</Note>

<Info>
Soon you'll be able to receive your Morning Briefing on other channels like WhatsApp! Email delivery is currently available.
</Info>

## How It Works

Every morning between 7:30-8:00 AM, Dume.ai's AI assistant:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gathers Your Information">
    The assistant collects data from multiple sources:
    - **Unread emails** from your inbox
    - **Calendar events** for the upcoming day
    - **Weather information** for your location
    - **News articles** relevant to your profession and interests
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyzes and Prioritizes">
    Using advanced AI, it identifies:
    - Urgent and high-priority emails
    - Important meetings and appointments
    - Key news stories relevant to you
    - Weather conditions that might affect your day
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compiles Your Briefing">
    All information is organized into a clean, easy-to-read email with:
    - Personalized weather forecast
    - Today's complete schedule
    - Top priorities from your emails and tasks
    - Urgent emails requiring immediate attention
    - Curated news snippets matching your professional interests
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivers to Your Inbox">
    Your personalized Morning Briefing arrives in your email inbox, ready to review over coffee or during your commute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Disable Morning Briefing

If you prefer not to receive daily briefings:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to System Workflows">
    Go to the [Workflows](https://app.dume.ai/workflow) page in Dume.ai and select the **System Workflows** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Morning Briefing">
    Locate the **Morning Briefing** workflow in the list of system workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Off">
    Click the toggle switch or **Disable** button to stop daily briefings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You'll no longer receive Morning Briefings. You can re-enable it anytime by following the same steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
When you disable Morning Briefing, you'll stop receiving daily digest emails. You can still manually access your email, calendar, and news through their respective integrations.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What time will I receive my Morning Briefing?">
    Your Morning Briefing is delivered daily between 7:30-8:00 AM in your local timezone. The exact time may vary slightly based on data processing.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I change the delivery time?">
    Custom delivery times are coming soon! Currently, all briefings are sent between 7:30-8:00 AM.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Will I receive briefings on weekends?">
    Yes, Morning Briefing is sent every day by default, including weekends.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How does the AI choose which emails are urgent?">
    The AI analyzes factors like sender importance, subject keywords, deadlines mentioned, and your past email interactions to determine urgency and priority.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Does this work with email providers other than Gmail?">
    Morning Briefing currently supports Gmail only. Support for Microsoft 365 and other email providers is in development.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can I receive briefings on WhatsApp instead of email?">
    WhatsApp delivery is coming soon! Currently, Morning Briefings are delivered via email only.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="What if I don't have any calendar events for the day?">
    Your briefing will still include weather, priority emails, and news. It will note that you have no scheduled events and might suggest time for focused work.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How is the news personalized?">
    News is curated based on your industry, professional role, and reading patterns that the AI learns over time.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="How can I change my weather location or temperature units?">
    Weather location is auto-detected based on your IP address. If it's incorrect or you want to change the temperature units (°F/°C), visit Settings and update your preferences. See the [Personalization guide](/getting-started/personalisation) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Absolutely. Dume.ai uses OAuth for secure authentication and encrypts all data. We process information in real-time and don't store sensitive content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
